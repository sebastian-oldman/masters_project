Colocation Insights
Colocation requirements of 10 MW or more of contiguous space are commanding a premium due to limited availability.
Thorough planning for capacity is crucial for colocation providers and occupiers to establish fair market values.

Market Trends
Pricing continues to surpass other North American markets, driven by tight vacancy rates.
New construction activity remained limited by power challenges.

Notable Activity
Several projects have been preleased and are slated for completion in H1 2026.
Silicon Valley Power's 4% rate increase took effect in January 2026.
Amazon Data Services secured approval for a 56-acre data center development in Gilroy.
